About ShortLifeMail

Built to protect your real inbox

ShortLifeMail is a free temporary email service. It was built for one purpose: to give people a simple, no-signup way to receive emails online without handing over their personal address.

What ShortLifeMail is

ShortLifeMail is a disposable inbox generator. When you open the site, it creates a temporary email address for you automatically. You can copy that address, use it on another website, and check your inbox by clicking Refresh. You don't need to sign up or choose a password.

The timer on this page starts at ten minutes, and you can extend it. When it ends, this page stops checking for new email. The timer does not delete the mail.tm account or its messages.

Why it exists

Many websites ask for an email address before showing content, unlocking a free download, or confirming a sign-up. In many cases, that email address then ends up on a mailing list that sends weekly newsletters, promotional emails, or marketing campaigns you never asked for.

ShortLifeMail was built to solve that problem. It gives you a real, working inbox that you can use for low-risk online tasks. You get the email you need. Your personal inbox stays clean.

No sign-up required. ShortLifeMail does not ask for your name or real email address to create an inbox. Technical data such as your IP address is still processed when you use the site. See our Privacy Policy.

What we believe about email privacy

Your personal email address is connected to your identity, your accounts, and your communication. Sharing it with every website you visit creates real risk: more spam, more marketing, and more exposure if a website is ever breached or sells its data.

We think people should have a practical way to manage that risk. Temporary email is not a perfect solution to every privacy problem, but it is a useful tool for reducing unnecessary exposure during routine online tasks.

What we stand for

No sign-up

You do not have to register to use ShortLifeMail. The site creates the temporary mail.tm account for you.

Clear about advertising

ShortLifeMail uses Google advertising technology, which can use cookies and similar technologies. Our code does not record which websites you use a temporary address on.

Short-term by design

Our code does not save your messages. mail.tm stores accounts and messages under its own policies, and the timer on this page does not delete them.

Honest about limits

We are clear about what temporary email is not suitable for. It should not be used for important, sensitive, or long-term accounts.

When to use ShortLifeMail

ShortLifeMail is best for low-risk, short-term situations. If you need to receive a confirmation email, access a free download, test a website, or sign up for something you may not use again, this tool can protect your main inbox from the follow-up.

  • Testing websites or web applications.
  • Receiving one-time confirmation emails.
  • Accessing free content without joining mailing lists.
  • Protecting your main inbox from spam.
  • Quick sign-ups where you do not need long-term access.

When not to use it

Temporary email is the wrong tool for accounts that matter. If you need to recover access to an account, receive sensitive information, or keep a record of what was sent, use your real email address.

  • Banking, financial accounts, or payments.
  • Medical, legal, or government services.
  • Password recovery for important accounts.
  • Shopping orders you need to track or return.
  • Any account you expect to use long-term.
