    Frequently asked questions

    Is ShortLifeMail free?

    Yes. You can create a temporary email address without signing up or paying.

    Why do I need to click Refresh?

    ShortLifeMail uses manual refresh to reduce unnecessary server requests and keep the tool lightweight. Wait around 20–30 seconds after an email is sent, then click Refresh.

    Can I use this email forever?

    No. Temporary emails are short-life inboxes. If you need a permanent inbox, use a normal email provider.

    Why did an email not arrive?

    Some websites block temporary email addresses. Delivery can also take time or fail depending on the sender. Try waiting a little longer and clicking Refresh again.

    Is temporary email safe?

    It is useful for low-risk sign-ups and spam protection, but it is not suitable for sensitive or important messages. Never use temporary email for banking, medical, legal, government, or password recovery emails.
