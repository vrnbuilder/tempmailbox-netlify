Privacy Policy

ShortLifeMail is designed to help you create temporary email addresses quickly. This policy explains what information may be processed when you use our website, and which other services are involved.

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Simple version: ShortLifeMail does not ask you to create an account, and we do not require your name, password, phone number, or personal email address to use the basic temporary inbox tool. Temporary inboxes are not private mailboxes. Emails are handled by mail.tm, a separate service, and the timer on our homepage does not delete them. Google advertising technology runs on this website.

1. Who we are

ShortLifeMail is operated by CLICK CLICK STORE LTD, a private limited company with company number 14290914. Its registered office is 224 Church Road, London, England, NW10 9NP. You can check these details on the Companies House register. In this policy, "we", "us" and "our" mean CLICK CLICK STORE LTD.

This policy covers ShortLifeMail at shortlifemail.com, our primary website. The old address tempmail.instantimagetools.com now redirects visitors to shortlifemail.com and no longer runs a separate copy of the website or email service. This policy does not cover other websites.

ShortLifeMail provides a temporary email tool that allows users to generate short-life email addresses for receiving messages. The website is intended for privacy, testing, sign-up verification, and reducing spam in your personal inbox.

2. Information you provide

You can use ShortLifeMail without creating an account. We do not ask for your name, phone number, home address, password, or personal email address to generate a temporary email address.

If you contact us directly, we may receive the information you choose to send, such as your email address, name, and message content. For privacy questions, email privacy@shortlifemail.com.

Emails sent to privacy@shortlifemail.com are forwarded by our email provider, Namecheap, to a Gmail mailbox. This means Namecheap and Google also process those messages.

3. Temporary email messages

Temporary inboxes are not private personal mailboxes. They are designed for short-term use only. Do not use ShortLifeMail for sensitive information such as banking, medical, legal, government, identity, password recovery, or confidential business messages.

When you open the homepage, our server creates an account with mail.tm, a separate temporary email service, and gets a temporary access token for it. The account's generated password is not shown to you and is not kept by our code. Your address and token are held in your browser while the page is open. If you reload the page or create a new address, this page loses access to the previous inbox.

Anyone who knows a temporary address can send email to it. When you refresh your inbox or open a message, the request and the message pass through our hosting provider, Netlify, so they can be shown on the page. Your access token is included in these requests. Our code does not save messages. Email is shown as plain text: remote images are not loaded, links do not open from the page, and attachments are not shown.

The ten-minute timer runs in your browser. When it ends, this page stops checking for new email. Extending it only changes the timer on this page. The timer does not delete the mail.tm account or its messages, and ShortLifeMail does not send delete requests to mail.tm.

ShortLifeMail does not guarantee message delivery, storage, privacy, or availability.

4. Cookies and similar technologies

Our own website code does not set cookies or use your browser's local storage. Google's advertising and consent technology on this website can use cookies and similar technologies.

This website does not currently show a cookie consent message. Google's consent technology can still store a cookie named FCCDCF on your device to record consent settings. In our own tests it was set to last about 13 months.

5. Advertising and Google AdSense

Google AdSense code is included on the pages of this website. Ads may not always be shown, but this code can still connect your browser to Google.

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's previous visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to ShortLifeMail and/or other sites on the Internet.

Users may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings. Users may also be able to opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising by visiting aboutads.info.

You can read how Google uses data on sites that use its services.

6. Analytics

Our website code does not include a separate analytics script.

7. Server logs

Like most websites, our hosting provider, Netlify, may automatically process basic technical information such as IP address, browser type, device type, referring page, date/time of visit, and pages requested. This is used for security, debugging, abuse prevention, and website operation.

8. How we use information

  • To provide and operate the temporary email tool.
  • To display inbox messages requested by the user.
  • To improve website performance, design, and reliability.
  • To prevent abuse, spam, attacks, or misuse of the service.
  • To respond to messages sent through our contact page.
  • To support advertising where allowed by law.

9. What you should not use ShortLifeMail for

You should not use ShortLifeMail for anything important, confidential, illegal, harmful, abusive, or long-term. Temporary email addresses can expire, stop working, or become inaccessible.

  • Do not use it for banking or financial accounts.
  • Do not use it for medical, legal, or government services.
  • Do not use it for password recovery.
  • Do not use it to send or receive illegal, abusive, or harmful content.
  • Do not use it where you need permanent access to an inbox.

10. Third-party services

ShortLifeMail relies on these services, which handle information under their own privacy policies:

  • Netlify: hosting and the server code that connects to mail.tm. Netlify privacy policy.
  • mail.tm: temporary email accounts and messages. mail.tm privacy policy.
  • Google: advertising and consent technology, and Gmail, which receives forwarded privacy emails. Google privacy policy.
  • Namecheap: email forwarding for privacy@shortlifemail.com.
  • Tailwind CSS content delivery network: styling on the homepage.

These services can receive technical information such as your IP address. The homepage loads code from Google and the Tailwind CSS network. Because that code runs on the same page as your inbox, it can technically access what the page displays. We do not deliberately send your messages or access token to Google or the Tailwind CSS network.

11. Data retention

Our code does not save your messages. mail.tm controls how long accounts and messages are kept, and the timer on our homepage does not delete them. We cannot confirm how long mail.tm keeps them. Please read mail.tm's own FAQ and privacy policy. Do not rely on ShortLifeMail for permanent message storage.

12. Your choices

13. Children's privacy

ShortLifeMail is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
